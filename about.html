My Journey

I am a Senior Risk Manager and Data Engineer based in Geneva. My professional trajectory lies at the intersection of academic scientific research, the private financial sector, and complex administrative management.

I design and engineer the entire data lifecycle: from cleaning and validating raw flows to building automated ETL/ELT pipelines, up to quantitative modeling and creating executive dashboards for decision support. My goal is to transform data "noise" into structured, compliant, and predictive information.
